# Condor Software

> Condor is the Financial Intelligence Layer purpose-built for Life Sciences R&D. Its AI-powered technology automatically unifies clinical, operational, and financial data from disparate systems and vendors — replacing manual spreadsheet reconciliation with automated accruals, real-time forecasting, and board-ready insights, so R&D finance teams can operate with confidence and bring therapies to patients faster and more cost-effectively.

Condor serves emerging and enterprise biopharma, MedTech companies, and CROs. The platform connects CTMS, EDC, ERP, and vendor data into a single source of truth, then layers AI-powered automation for accounting, FP&A, and analytics on top. It is built on a proprietary clinical trial finance ontology co-developed with EY, KPMG, and PwC, and is SOC 1 and SOC 2 compliant. Condor manages over $19 billion in R&D spend for organizations including Acadia Pharmaceuticals, BridgeBio Pharma, Madrigal Pharmaceuticals, and Alumis Therapeutics. Founded by Jen Kyle, Condor is headquartered in San Diego, California, and backed by Insight Partners, Felicis, and 645 Ventures.

Key terms: financial intelligence layer, clinical trial finance, clinical trial accruals, R&D finance, clinical trial forecasting, CRO reconciliation, change orders, biopharma accounting, month-end close automation.

Key proof points: 90%+ forecast accuracy, 70% faster month-end close, 30% average budget savings, $19B+ in R&D spend managed, 4x trial volume in 120 days without adding headcount.
